- **Data Classification and Visualisation 1: Classifying Data and Basic Graphs** (p10-guided-practice): New teaching: Question 4b relies on comparing values read from different scales rather than comparing bar lengths, which the column-graph lesson never teaches. Add a short example showing this.
- **Algebra 1: Introduction to Algebra** (p16-guided): New teaching: The lesson only goes from a picture to an expression. Questions 8 and 9 (parts g to j) ask students to draw an expression such as $4x+2$, and no example shows how. Add a worked example of drawing groups and loose items.
- **Linear Relationships 1A: Tables, Rules and Graphs** (page-5-q1): New teaching: The booklet teaches only whole-number plotting, but later questions plot and read points such as (1.5, -4), (-1/2, 3) and (1/3, 5). Add a short example showing where points between grid lines sit.
- **Probability** (p5-describe): New teaching: Add a short taught step on comparing outcomes as 'n times as likely' by comparing counts of equal sectors, including a non-whole-number multiple. Q4 to Q6 depend on it and nothing before them teaches it.
- **Properties of Geometrical Figures** (p36-example): New teaching: The worked examples never show a reflex interior angle, but several exercise parts need it. Add an example using the reflex angle at full size and finding it from an outside angle.
- **Rates 2: Speed, Time and Distance–Time Graphs** (p42-car-block): New teaching: The lesson teaches reading distance-time graphs, but p47-q11 asks students to draw one from a description. A worked example of building the graph (cumulative distances, clock times, scales) is missing.
- **Rates 2: Speed, Time and Distance–Time Graphs** (p49-guided-question): New teaching: The lesson teaches speed as a positive magnitude, but p53-q7 needs a signed rate of change for a falling water level. A worked example is missing.
- **Ratios 1: Equivalent and Simplified Ratios** (p6-q6): Bridge: Q9c asks students to compare four ratios as fractions over a common denominator, and nothing before it practises that. Add a short scaffolded comparison of two part:part ratios, such as 1:3 and 2:5, after Q6 and before Q9.
- **Equations 1B: Expanding, Both Sides and Problem Solving** (p66-guided): New teaching: Question 9 relies on reading a rod diagram to find how many of one rod equal another (for example p = 4w) and then rewriting expressions in one rod length. Nothing earlier teaches this, so a short worked lead-in is needed before it.
- **Indices 1: Index Notation, Divisibility and Primes** (p20-q16-block): Bridge: Students move from Yes/No divisibility checks to open 'explain why' questions with no scaffold. Add a short sentence-completion question before question 17 that links multiples of 4 to multiples of 2.
- **Volume** (p3-guided): New teaching: Students are asked to build a prism from its views, but the examples and guided practice only go from solid to views. Add a worked example of reading the views and sketching the prism.
- **Volume** (p25-q3): Variation: A run holding radius and length fixed while the fraction goes whole, half, quarter, sixth would show the volume is that fraction of the full cylinder. Question 5 changes several features at once and does not isolate this.
- **Volume** (p24-guided): Applied automatically · New teaching: Finding a radius or height from a given volume is never shown for cylinders. Add a worked example that substitutes into V = πr²h and rearranges.
