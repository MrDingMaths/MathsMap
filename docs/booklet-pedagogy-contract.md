# Booklet teaching and atomisation contract

Companion to the graph, teaching, worked-example and guided-practice principles. Use the [current workflow](booklet-human-workflow.md) and [direct import evidence requirements](booklet-direct-compact-import.md). Booklet authoring does not authorise automatic public skill changes.

## Evidence and grain

Use the supplied percentage operations, index laws and non-right-angled trigonometry exemplars as calibration evidence. Review theory and the complete practice range before deciding that coverage is complete. Record exact source pages and blocks for each decision. Existing accepted and rejected decisions in booklets/proposals remain binding precedents.

A teaching atom is an independently teachable or assessable step, recognition decision, fact or transformation. A public graph skill is a separate curriculum-planning decision. A step may deserve a recognition drill or a prerequisite review without deserving a new public graph node. Retain teaching dependencies in the audit even when the graph policy intentionally omits ambient skills; the graph omission policy applies to graph edges, not to evidence needed by a teacher.

Write the routine line by line and identify what permits each transition, including invisible decisions. Mark familiar prerequisites; present unfamiliar atoms before asking students to combine them. Chain two or three atoms before the full routine when the intermediate has educational value. Do not manufacture procedural steps for pure recognition.

## Examples that reveal the essential feature

Simple numbers reduce incidental arithmetic. They must not hide the procedure or allow a wrong method to give the same answer. Prefer distinct numerical roles and a general first case; special cases become deliberate later contrasts. Ask what plausible wrong method would succeed on this example before accepting it.

The percentage exemplar's 35% of 240 supports the conversion-and-multiplication procedure with a check on whether the result is smaller or larger than the original. Contrast percentages below, above and below one percent after the procedure is established. Changes in the base quantity must remain consistent across wording and working; the source itself is not infallible.

The index-law recognition sequence distinguishes equal bases from different bases and multiplication from addition, including implicit exponent one. These are essential conditions, not merely different numbers. Preserve near-miss non-examples and avoid confusing the equal-base multiplication law with other valid laws for equal exponents.

The trigonometry review separates opposite side–angle identification from solving a proportion. The complete routine then needs an applicability decision, correct pair selection, substitution, rearrangement and interpretation. A rearrangement drill can be a teaching atom without adding every algebraic microstep to the public graph.

For category teaching use the existing NPPPN framework: near non-example, minimally changed positive, varied positives, and a boundary non-example. Mix recognition practice so position does not reveal the answer. Change one critical feature at a time in comparison sets. Keep wording consistent when wording is not the feature under study.

Guided practice uses the same underlying procedure with changed surface features. Fade scaffolding; preserve the worked example as an accessible reference. Any extension that introduces a new requirement must identify it rather than treating it as familiar. Explanations and checks belong beside corresponding working, not on every equals line.

## Coverage and sequence

Scope a booklet to selected NSW content groups and explicit extensions. The bank contains the union of source and MathsMap coverage; missing mappings or archetypes are visible gaps. Do not silently discard unmatched material or force approximate one-to-one mappings.

Use theory, guided practice and blocked practice per teaching chunk; mini mixed reviews follow intermediate chunks, followed by a cumulative interleaved set and challenge. Mastery is an optional strand. Every applicable archetype/tier requires coverage; record a rationale for non-applicable tiers. Difficulty comes from mathematical demand, not merely larger numbers or longer prose.

Interleaving tests method selection. Draw only on taught skills/prerequisites, balance covered chunks, avoid unintended repeats, and preserve shared stems, diagrams and dependent parts as selection units. Imported questions are candidates for cumulative practice, not automatically suitable because of their origin.

## Diagrams and corrections

Preserve source mathematical structure. Use known equations/domains for graphs, known constraints for geometry, and common base geometry for question/solution overlays. Never fit a decorative curve where an equation is known or invent an equation where it is not. Keep source images when reconstruction is uncertain; flag contradictions.

Record source evidence and rationale for corrections. Preserve original evidence, saved edits and question identities. Verify content, teaching/arrangement fidelity and rendered layout separately, against the current revision. Unresolved conflicts remain review findings. Apply confirmed source corrections only within the user's authorised scope, retaining the original and replacement; do not silently copy or change source errors.

## House style 1.1

Graph typography after final scaling targets 8.5 pt axis numbers (8 pt only when crowded) and 10 pt axis, coordinate and equation labels. Preserve source numerical scales and tick marks, including their absence, independently of grids. Verify final-size readability and collisions. Use grey grids, solid black axes, and blue/red/green successive plots. Descriptive first columns in value tables must fit one line at normal text size. Cloze and response spaces must match the expected handwritten response, with multiple lines for sentences. No accepted page may intrude within 3 mm of the footer; reflow or continue rather than reducing readability or writing capacity. See [the complete requirements](booklet-house-style-1.1.md).
