# Standing booklet conventions

Moved word for word from `AGENTS.md` on 9 October 2026, so that file stays under the 32,768 bytes Codex loads. `AGENTS.md` tells every agent to read this page before booklet or question-bank work. The paragraphs keep their original order and wording; only link paths changed. Newer dated rules in `AGENTS.md` take precedence where they say so.

Short-answer precedence (16 September 2026): all current and future compact back-of-book answers use two 9 pt columns with an 8 mm gutter and measured three/two/one-entry rows inside each column, with 3 mm between cells. Preserve full labels and reading order. Explanations, brief methods, tables and diagrams span the column. Remove grey divider rules between questions and parts; keep exercise separators and meaningful table/diagram borders. Harder answers retain their final result and add a concise method where useful, following the booklet's taught approach and the worked-solution contract. Keep result and method in editable `answer.short`; preserve worked solutions and question pagination settings.

Diagrams are unshaded by default across all current booklets, bank content and future transcriptions. Retain only restrained shading with a specific mathematical purpose or a demonstrated final-size clarity benefit; source shading alone is insufficient. Preserve structural white masks, markers and meaningful graph regions. Follow the [purposeful shading contract](booklet-diagram-colours.md#purposeful-shading-12-september-2026), review every occurrence, and pass `scripts/booklet/check-diagram-shading.mjs` with current source-hashed evidence before accepting imports, edits or restores.

Legacy-profile final edition-review precedence (16 September 2026): for runs without the three-pass profile, follow [the unique-layout acceptance policy](booklet-review-first-workflow.md#unique-layout-final-acceptance). All five export formats still receive required automated checks. Visually inspect the three standalone layouts (`student`, `short`, `worked`); combined page bodies may reuse those actual inspections only with current, exact PDF-pixel comparison evidence. Inspect changed/unmatched pages, combined covers and answer-section boundaries, and explicitly review covers, contents, transitions, numbering, every footer and links in both combined editions. No source/DOM hash or automatic pass establishes visual inspection. A new settlement requires fresh standalone inspection and current comparison/composition evidence. This supersedes earlier requirements to visually reread identical pages in all five editions, including the trig-specific wording below. Full manual five-edition review remains a supported fallback.

Apply the [Volume feedback prevention rules](booklet-volume-feedback-2026-09-12.md#prevention-in-future-work) to future imports, edits and restores. Preserve source-relative diagram/working/photo placement and meaningful paired figures; review scaffold baselines, complete formula annotations and cross-section orientation at final size. Use shared section headers, vertical-alignment controls and dotted question scaffolds. Volume's local widths and placements are examples, not universal dimensions. Presentation readiness rejects explicit underlined-space blanks; regression tests audit current project/bank content. Geometry and visual review remain separate gates.

Legacy-profile transcription runs use the [review-first gates](booklet-review-first-workflow.md): early mathematical consistency review with bundled editorial decisions, final-size representative-pattern acceptance before bulk authoring, and one structured correction/issue register with automatic propagation. Retain redundant measurements; being unused is not a defect, and stated precision matters. Validate triangle constructions numerically and appearance separately. During development inspect affected pages and pagination neighbours; retain independent inventory and complete final edition coverage under the unique-layout acceptance policy after content settles. Do not claim measured token/time savings without actual run metrics.

Use the accepted compact exercise style for all future booklets: flexible pagination, one numbered exercise per topic, continuous question numbering within each exercise across teaching checkpoints, editor-only difficulty ratings, two-column 9 pt short answers with an 8 mm gutter, and single-column 9.5 pt worked solutions. Use consistent answer-label gutters, bounded readable diagrams and calculated contents/question/answer navigation. Default to Questions and short answers. See [the compact exercise specification](booklet-compact-exercise-trial.md). Preserve existing projects unless their migration is requested.

For booklet generation and solution editing, read [the worked-solution contract](booklet-worked-solution-style.md). Use MathsDatabase notation and concise student-facing voice, and use the methods taught in the booklet's worked examples, Key Ideas and scaffolds. Obtain missing teaching context or flag it for review; do not silently substitute a different method.

New compact imports must use Linear v1's teaching-group header/box templates, with one header per group and no generated activity references. Both answer editions are practice-only; teaching-answer controls retain the teaching responses. Use source-supported response requirements and meaningful grid arrangements, with no extra space for cloze-only Key Ideas. Content fidelity, teaching/arrangement fidelity and rendered layout must pass separately; counts and absence of overflow are insufficient. See the direct compact workflow for `sourceReview` evidence and cache requirements.

For future booklets, front-cover contents entries must omit the word “Exercise”. For numbered exercises, use separate columns: exercise number, topic title at a consistent tab stop, and right-aligned page number, with no dot leaders or separator symbol. Retain navigation links. Exercise headings inside the booklet may still use “Exercise”. The shared renderer in `src/lib/booklet-cover.js` and `src/components/BookletCover.svelte` implements this convention. The small question-side “Answers” navigation link is screen-only and must be hidden in print.

For all future booklet covers, omit the syllabus-summary lines beneath the book number. Align the “Book 1” (or other book number) text with the main title and contents heading. Position version and feedback together on the right, but keep the text within that block left-aligned. Include linked “Short answers” and/or “Worked solutions” contents entries only when those sections are included in the selected edition. Show each answer-section heading only at the section's start, while retaining exercise context on subsequent pages.

The canonical Linear Relationships booklet is **Linear Relationships v1**, `booklets/projects/linear-relationships-v1.json`, copied from Compact Exercise Trial revision 8. Preserve its questions and compact layout unless the user requests changes. The predecessors (Linear Relationships v0, Flexible and Compact Exercise Trial) are retained locally under `.booklet-work/recovery/`; required compatibility fixtures remain in `tests/fixtures/booklets/`. Saving an original booklet automatically syncs its question content and worked solutions through `saveBookletProject`; ownership/baselines are stored in `booklets/question-bank/.sync/links.json`. Keep classifications, teaching mappings, stable IDs and local layout. Conflicting content must pause that question's sync for explicit review. Other booklets stay pinned until their user accepts an update. Direct file-edit maintenance must reconcile bank changes explicitly through the shared bank transaction helpers. See `docs/booklet-bank-auto-sync.md`.

Preserve source activity identity, meaningful text/equation colours, speech bubbles, native cards, equation alignment and writable-box dimensions in new imports. Red activities with demonstrations and responses keep one source heading; do not split them into orange Example/Guided Practice groups. Use the shared editable speech-bubble and card templates. Writing boxes must remain large enough for handwriting even in superscripts/fractions. Check repeated occurrences across the whole source. Index Laws uses explicitly requested source page boundaries; compact answer pagination and other projects' pagination defaults remain unchanged. See the direct compact workflow for verifier v2 presentation evidence.

Ordinary general-diagram lines and labels use solid black `#000000` across all current and future booklets and editions. Retain semantic colours and meaningful fills; graphs retain their separate palette rules. Source hue alone is not semantic evidence. Follow [the shared diagram colour contract](booklet-diagram-colours.md); this instruction overrides earlier decorative source-palette preservation for diagram outlines and ordinary labels.

## Diagram typography and ordinary short-answer ink (11 September 2026)

Implementation and completed acceptance evidence: [diagram typography contract](booklet-diagram-typography.md).

Every complete native diagram label is **10 pt at final printed size (tolerance 0.1 pt)**, independent of diagram width. The shared TikZ path calibrates whole labels, retaining relative superscripts/subscripts, fractions, rotation and alignment. Graph ticks retain 8.5 pt, or explicitly reviewed 8 pt exceptions. Do not repeatedly scale node fonts. Resolve collisions by label placement or available space; preserve mathematical content, mathematical colour meaning through the standard palette and meaningful arrangements. Raster labels need separate review. Check editing, preview, repeated resizing, page zoom, cached/fresh rendering, save/reopen, answer widths and PDF.

Ordinary practice short-answer prose, native mathematics, answer numbers and part labels inherit **#24282d**, including structured editor content. Do not embed decorative blue. Retain colour with mathematical meaning (series identification and correctness symbols); teaching-page answers, worked solutions and diagram palettes are outside this ink change. Both answer sections remain practice-only. Repair existing content through the revision-safe project/bank transaction, preserving IDs, classifications, local layouts, current edits and source evidence. Record these intentional departures from source appearance. Preserve existing pagination settings. Inspect affected pages and neighbours, then all five trig editions and affected pages elsewhere; check collisions, clipping, writing space, footer clearance and navigation.

Current palette precedence (11 September 2026): all editable content in every current booklet, bank and future transcription uses the [standard booklet palette](booklet-standard-palette.md). Preserve mathematical meaning through standard accents, rather than retaining source shades. This includes teaching responses, worked solutions, fills and backgrounds. Retain original evidence and raster pixels; restored projects require current palette acceptance before export.

Main section-header precedence (11 September 2026): across all current booklets and future transcriptions, main section bands use desaturated blue `#52769a` with white `#ffffff` text through the shared `headerBlue` token. Preserve existing header geometry. Light teaching-group headers and unfilled exercise/answer headings retain near-black text; mathematical blue remains `#268cff`. This overrides the earlier blanket near-black heading rule in palette standardisation.

## Shared-stem question groups and working space (17 September 2026)

Group related source items under their shared instruction or definition as one editable question with a, b, c parts and a native grid, even when the source numbers them individually. Preserve meaningful range, exercise and category boundaries, nested parts and original source identities in provenance. Renumber displayed questions within each exercise. Generate/review worked solutions first and estimate per-part handwriting space from mathematical steps (including TeX rows), cell-width wrapping, fractions and required drawings; save editable estimates and retain manual overrides. Inspect representative grids at final size, including pagination. Do not repeat the stem per item or use question-type allowances alone. See [practice-only import contract](booklet-practice-only-import.md).

## Short-answer rows, wrapper labels and labels beside layout blocks (11 October 2026, user requested)

These apply to every booklet through the shared renderer; no booklet content is edited for them.

- **Multipart questions start a new short-answer row.** Parts of one question may share a row with each other (`3i 3j`), but never with another question (`4a` starts the next row). Questions with a single answer may still share (`3 4 5`). A question continued across blocks counts as one question. `packShortAnswerRows` in `src/lib/booklet-short-answer-grid.js` enforces this.
- **Layout-only wrappers add no label segment.** A group with a blank label and no prompt that only arranges labelled parts (for example two source columns holding parts a–h) is invisible in answer labels: `4a`, not `4(1)a`. A wrapper keeps its position number when it has its own heading, contains an unlabelled response, or its part labels repeat elsewhere in the same parent (`4(1)a`, `4(2)a`). For new imports, prefer `label: null` or no wrapper for pure layout groups.
- **A part label shares the first line of a leading side-by-side block.** When a part's prompt opens with a borderless, untitled `parallel`, `scaffold`, `worked-rows` or `investigation` layout block, the block's top margin and top padding render as zero directly after the label, and the label shares its first text baseline. Space below, side padding, bordered or titled blocks, cards and speech bubbles are unchanged. Do not set `margin: 0, padding: 0` on such blocks merely to close this gap.
- **A missing numerator or denominator is a box inside a native fraction** (11 October 2026, user requested). Write it as one maths fraction with `\boxed{\rule{0pt}{5mm}\hspace{Wmm}}` for each missing part, as in Algebraic Fractions p5 Q1, with `W` from the blank's handwriting estimate (8 mm minimum). Do not build the fraction from dotted blanks stacked around a drawn `\rule` bar. This is a standing exception to dots-by-default. Blanks that hold quantities with units (Rates p21) stay dotted.
- **Exception: teaching blocks that fill blanks in place keep dotted fractions.** A box cannot carry an answer. Where a review, guided-practice, activity or example block shows its answers inside its own blanks (it keeps its prompt beside the answers, or uses table responses), leave the missing numerator or denominator as dotted blanks, as in Right-Angled Trigonometry p25 Review. Practice questions and teaching blocks that list their answers separately use the box.
