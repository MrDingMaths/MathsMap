# MathsMap working conventions

Diagrams are unshaded by default across all current booklets, bank content and future transcriptions. Retain only restrained shading with a specific mathematical purpose or a demonstrated final-size clarity benefit; source shading alone is insufficient. Preserve structural white masks, markers and meaningful graph regions. Follow the [purposeful shading contract](docs/booklet-diagram-colours.md#purposeful-shading-12-september-2026), review every occurrence, and pass `scripts/booklet/check-diagram-shading.mjs` with current source-hashed evidence before accepting imports, edits or restores.

For all 3D diagrams, follow [the visibility contract](docs/solid-visibility.md). Use explicit solid faces and the actual viewing direction to derive hidden edges; silhouettes stay solid. Labels use non-drawing paths and cannot repaint edges. Preserve construction/semantic lines separately. Audit every current occurrence and new candidate with `scripts/audit-solid-visibility.mjs`; unresolved geometry requires a source-hashed review. Changing camera or coordinates invalidates prior visibility acceptance.

For booklet changes, follow [the change runbook](docs/booklet-change-runbook.md): establish affected scope once, validate representative cases early, reuse valid renders, settle changes before final exports, run required final checks once, and record actual phase timings/retries. Preserve all existing fidelity and final visual-review gates; use affected-page/neighbour checks during development and avoid duplicate full exports.

Earlier-session rules are consolidated in [the cross-session transcription and rendering rules](docs/booklet-cross-session-rules.md). Read them with the feedback checklist for every future transcription and rendering change. They distinguish general conventions from source-specific corrections and document precedence where old prompts disagree.

User feedback is a general requirement across all books and every applicable occurrence, including existing books, unless the user explicitly limits it to a specific item. Implement shared renderer/editor causes centrally; audit and repair repeated content defects throughout the affected booklet. Do not treat a named example as the full scope or leave known repeated defects as a list in place of fixing them. Preserve book-specific content, mathematical colour meaning through the standard palette and meaningful arrangements while applying general feedback. Fraction-step spacing is shared across all books and answer editions, not an Index Laws content patch.

For every transcription run, read and complete the [transcription feedback acceptance checklist](docs/booklet-transcription-feedback-checklist.md). It consolidates the user's alignment, spacing, palette, grid, card, native-maths, writable-box, numbered-list and image-control feedback. Check every applicable occurrence against the source and rendered output before reporting completion; fix shared causes and retain regression coverage rather than waiting for the user to identify repeated defects.

Apply the [Volume feedback prevention rules](docs/booklet-volume-feedback-2026-09-12.md#prevention-in-future-work) to future imports, edits and restores. Preserve source-relative diagram/working/photo placement and meaningful paired figures; review scaffold baselines, complete formula annotations and cross-section orientation at final size. Use shared section headers, vertical-alignment controls and dotted question scaffolds. Volume's local widths and placements are examples, not universal dimensions. Presentation readiness rejects explicit underlined-space blanks; regression tests audit current project/bank content. Geometry and visual review remain separate gates.

For new imports, use the [direct compact workflow](docs/booklet-direct-compact-import.md): independent source inventory, semantic content with source references, then one compact editable project. Imported counts do not establish fidelity. Record confirmed source corrections without changing original evidence. Keep exact cloning optional. Projects is the sole booklet workspace. Historical-import browsing and creation are retired; new imports require explicit semantic candidates. Retain source serving, correction conflict checks and exact-mode compatibility.

New transcription runs also use the [review-first gates](docs/booklet-review-first-workflow.md): early mathematical consistency review with bundled editorial decisions, final-size representative-pattern acceptance before bulk authoring, and one structured correction/issue register with automatic propagation. Retain redundant measurements; being unused is not a defect, and stated precision matters. Validate triangle constructions numerically and appearance separately. During development inspect affected pages and pagination neighbours; retain independent inventory and the complete final five-edition visual review after content settles. Do not claim measured token/time savings without actual run metrics.

Use the accepted compact exercise style for all future booklets: flexible pagination, one numbered exercise per topic, continuous question numbering within each exercise across teaching checkpoints, editor-only difficulty ratings, two-column 9 pt short answers with an 8 mm gutter, and single-column 9.5 pt worked solutions. Use consistent answer-label gutters, bounded readable diagrams and calculated contents/question/answer navigation. Default to Questions and short answers. See [the compact exercise specification](docs/booklet-compact-exercise-trial.md). Preserve existing projects unless their migration is requested.

For booklet generation and solution editing, read [the worked-solution contract](docs/booklet-worked-solution-style.md). Use MathsDatabase notation and concise student-facing voice, and use the methods taught in the booklet's worked examples, Key Ideas and scaffolds. Obtain missing teaching context or flag it for review; do not silently substitute a different method.

New compact imports must use Linear v1's teaching-group header/box templates, with one header per group and no generated activity references. Both answer editions are practice-only; teaching-answer controls retain the teaching responses. Use source-supported response requirements and meaningful grid arrangements, with no extra space for cloze-only Key Ideas. Content fidelity, teaching/arrangement fidelity and rendered layout must pass separately; counts and absence of overflow are insufficient. See the direct compact workflow for `sourceReview` evidence and cache requirements.

For future booklets, front-cover contents entries must omit the word “Exercise”. For numbered exercises, use separate columns: exercise number, topic title at a consistent tab stop, and right-aligned page number, with no dot leaders or separator symbol. Retain navigation links. Exercise headings inside the booklet may still use “Exercise”. The shared renderer in `src/lib/booklet-cover.js` and `src/components/BookletCover.svelte` implements this convention. The small question-side “Answers” navigation link is screen-only and must be hidden in print.

For all future booklet covers, omit the syllabus-summary lines beneath the book number. Align the “Book 1” (or other book number) text with the main title and contents heading. Position version and feedback together on the right, but keep the text within that block left-aligned. Include linked “Short answers” and/or “Worked solutions” contents entries only when those sections are included in the selected edition. Show each answer-section heading only at the section's start, while retaining exercise context on subsequent pages.

The canonical Linear Relationships booklet is **Linear Relationships v1**, `booklets/projects/linear-relationships-v1.json`, copied from Compact Exercise Trial revision 8. Preserve its questions and compact layout unless the user requests changes. The predecessors (Linear Relationships v0, Flexible and Compact Exercise Trial) are retained locally under `.booklet-work/recovery/`; required compatibility fixtures remain in `tests/fixtures/booklets/`. Saving an original booklet automatically syncs its question content and worked solutions through `saveBookletProject`; ownership/baselines are stored in `booklets/question-bank/.sync/links.json`. Keep classifications, teaching mappings, stable IDs and local layout. Conflicting content must pause that question's sync for explicit review. Other booklets stay pinned until their user accepts an update. Direct file-edit maintenance must reconcile bank changes explicitly through the shared bank transaction helpers. See `docs/booklet-bank-auto-sync.md`.

Keep application code, tests, reusable authoring tools, current booklet/bank content and referenced assets in Git. Local render caches, extracted evidence, run logs and automatic project revision histories are not release files. Preserve original source documents and useful recovery/evidence records locally when cleaning temporary renders.

Follow [the storage policy](docs/booklet-storage.md): keep fast autosaving separate from five-minute recovery checkpoints; repeated fine adjustments must not create a whole-booklet backup on every save. Studio retains the latest 20 loose checkpoints plus explicit milestones and archives older snapshots with verified hashes. Keep the ignored `booklets/.storage-archives/` objects, manifests and receipts together. Never apply project-history pruning to bank baselines. Preview maintenance before applying it; check live references and completed-run receipts before archiving staging payloads. Run `npm run repo:check` before committing; keep generated files out of Git and review justified source/content growth against `booklets/storage-policy.json`.

Preserve source activity identity, meaningful text/equation colours, speech bubbles, native cards, equation alignment and writable-box dimensions in new imports. Red activities with demonstrations and responses keep one source heading; do not split them into orange Example/Guided Practice groups. Use the shared editable speech-bubble and card templates. Writing boxes must remain large enough for handwriting even in superscripts/fractions. Check repeated occurrences across the whole source. Index Laws uses explicitly requested source page boundaries; compact answer pagination and other projects' pagination defaults remain unchanged. See the direct compact workflow for verifier v2 presentation evidence.

Ordinary general-diagram lines and labels use solid black `#000000` across all current and future booklets and editions. Retain semantic colours and meaningful fills; graphs retain their separate palette rules. Source hue alone is not semantic evidence. Follow [the shared diagram colour contract](docs/booklet-diagram-colours.md); this instruction overrides earlier decorative source-palette preservation for diagram outlines and ordinary labels.

## Diagram typography and ordinary short-answer ink (11 September 2026)

Implementation and completed acceptance evidence: [diagram typography contract](docs/booklet-diagram-typography.md).

Every complete native diagram label is **10 pt at final printed size (tolerance 0.1 pt)**, independent of diagram width. The shared TikZ path calibrates whole labels, retaining relative superscripts/subscripts, fractions, rotation and alignment. Graph ticks retain 8.5 pt, or explicitly reviewed 8 pt exceptions. Do not repeatedly scale node fonts. Resolve collisions by label placement or available space; preserve mathematical content, mathematical colour meaning through the standard palette and meaningful arrangements. Raster labels need separate review. Check editing, preview, repeated resizing, page zoom, cached/fresh rendering, save/reopen, answer widths and PDF.

Ordinary practice short-answer prose, native mathematics, answer numbers and part labels inherit **#24282d**, including structured editor content. Do not embed decorative blue. Retain colour with mathematical meaning (series identification and correctness symbols); teaching-page answers, worked solutions and diagram palettes are outside this ink change. Both answer sections remain practice-only. Repair existing content through the revision-safe project/bank transaction, preserving IDs, classifications, local layouts, current edits and source evidence. Record these intentional departures from source appearance. Preserve existing pagination settings. Inspect affected pages and neighbours, then all five trig editions and affected pages elsewhere; check collisions, clipping, writing space, footer clearance and navigation.


Current palette precedence (11 September 2026): all editable content in every current booklet, bank and future transcription uses the [standard booklet palette](docs/booklet-standard-palette.md). Preserve mathematical meaning through standard accents, rather than retaining source shades. This includes teaching responses, worked solutions, fills and backgrounds. Retain original evidence and raster pixels; restored projects require current palette acceptance before export.


Main section-header precedence (11 September 2026): across all current booklets and future transcriptions, main section bands use desaturated blue `#52769a` with white `#ffffff` text through the shared `headerBlue` token. Preserve existing header geometry. Light teaching-group headers and unfilled exercise/answer headings retain near-black text; mathematical blue remains `#268cff`. This overrides the earlier blanket near-black heading rule in palette standardisation.
